How shipping works

  • Dispatch: in-stock orders are packed and handed to our courier within 1–2 business days.
  • Delivery time: most metro addresses receive orders in 2–5 business days; other pin codes can take a little longer.
  • Shipping fee: free on all orders above ₹999. Smaller orders show any shipping charge clearly before you place the order.
  • Tracking: every order page shows the current status, from pending to delivered.
  • Packaging: products are packed securely with protective, mostly paper-based materials.
{% elif page_key == 'returns' %}

30-day easy returns

  • Window: you can request a return within 30 days of delivery.
  • Condition: items should be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories included.
  • Start a return: sign in, open your order from “My orders”, and contact us with the order number.
  • Refunds: once the item is received and checked, the refund is processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Damaged or wrong item? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we will replace it or refund you in full.
{% elif page_key == 'privacy' %}

Your privacy, respected

  • What we collect: your name, email, delivery address and phone number — only what an order needs.
  • What we never do: we do not sell, rent or trade your personal information with third parties.
  • Passwords: stored only as secure one-way hashes; even our team cannot see them.
  • Cookies: used for sign-in sessions and your cart — nothing invasive.
  • Your control: contact us any time to review or delete the data linked to your account.
{% elif page_key == 'terms' %}

The straightforward terms

  • Pricing: all prices are shown in Indian Rupees (₹) and already include any listed discounts.
  • Availability: stock levels are shown live; an order is confirmed only after checkout succeeds.
  • Order snapshots: the price you pay is frozen on your order, even if a price changes later.
  • Accounts: keep your sign-in details private; you are responsible for orders placed from your account.
  • Fair use: we may cancel orders that abuse promotions or show fraudulent activity.
